## Filling in the week

Each row is a chore. Each column is a day. A box means the chore happens that day, and a tick means it's done.

All seven days start selected. Clear the ones that don't apply — the bins, if they only go out on Monday — and those squares stay empty.

The week starts on Monday. Switch it to Sunday if that's how yours runs. The columns and the rotation both follow the day you pick.

## Sharing the chores between the children

Turn on **Rotate chores between the children each week** and the chart shares the chores out, then moves each one on to the next child when the week changes. Two children and two chores means one each this week and a swap next week.

The link stays the same. Open it next week and the names have moved on, because the week is worked out from today's date.

Leave the switch off and a chore stays with the child you gave it to.

This is a chart for one week. Stuck to the fridge, it doesn't update itself.

## Print it, or tick it on a phone

**Download as PDF** opens your browser's print window. Choose Save as PDF, or print onto A4 or US Letter. The paper is black and white, and it's the chart on its own — the rest of this page stays on the website.

On a phone, swipe sideways for the later days. The boxes are sized to tap. Ticks are kept in the link and they clear when the week changes, so the next one starts blank.

Nothing here is uploaded. The names sit in the link, and anyone you send that link to can read them. Share it the way you'd share the paper on the fridge.
